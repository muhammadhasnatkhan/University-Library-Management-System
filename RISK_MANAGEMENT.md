# 6. Project Risk Management

## 1. Introduction

Risk management is an important part of project planning. It helps identify possible problems that may affect the project, assess their likelihood and impact, and develop strategies to reduce or manage them.

## 2. Risk Identification and Assessment

The following risks have been identified for the University Library Management System.

| No. | Identified Risk | Likelihood | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Unclear project requirements | Medium | High |
| 2 | Delay in project completion | High | High |
| 3 | Poor communication between team members | Medium | Medium |
| 4 | Limited technical resources | Medium | High |
| 5 | Changes in project requirements | Medium | Medium |
| 6 | Loss of project files or documents | Low | High |
| 7 | Lack of user involvement | Medium | Medium |
| 8 | Errors in system design or prototype | Medium | High |

## 3. Risk Mitigation Strategies

| No. | Identified Risk | Mitigation Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Unclear requirements | Discuss requirements carefully and collect necessary information. |
| 2 | Project delays | Prepare a schedule and monitor progress regularly. |
| 3 | Poor communication | Arrange regular meetings and share project updates. |
| 4 | Limited resources | Use available tools and resources effectively. |
| 5 | Changing requirements | Review and document changes before accepting them. |
| 6 | Loss of files | Keep backup copies of important project documents. |
| 7 | Lack of user involvement | Involve librarians and students in requirement gathering. |
| 8 | Design errors | Review system models and evaluate the prototype. |

## 4. Risk Management Responsibilities

- **Muhammad Hasnat Khan (Team Leader):** Monitor project risks, manage deadlines, and coordinate risk management activities.
- **Zarrar (Team Member):** Identify requirement-related risks and assist in finding solutions.

## 5. Conclusion

Identifying and managing project risks helps the team avoid unexpected problems and complete project activities successfully. Regular monitoring, communication, and proper planning will help reduce risks and achieve project objectives within the given time.
